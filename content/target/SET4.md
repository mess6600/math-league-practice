## Pair 1 (Questions 1–2) — 6 minutes

**1.** A movie theater has 18 rows with 22 seats in each row.  
If 45 seats are empty, how many seats are filled?

**2.** Find 40% of 85.

## Pair 2 (Questions 3–4) — 6 minutes

**3.** A rectangular prism (box) measures 6 cm by 4 cm by 3 cm.  
What is its surface area, in square centimeters?

**4.** Simplify: (3/4) ÷ (1/8)

## Pair 3 (Questions 5–6) — 6 minutes

**5.** Elena scores 88, 92, and 85 on three tests.  
What score does she need on the fourth test to average exactly 90?

**6.** A clock shows 2:15. What time will it show in 3 hours and 50 minutes?

## Pair 4 (Questions 7–8) — 6 minutes

**7.** The GCF of two numbers is 6. The LCM of the same two numbers is 60.  
One of the numbers is 12. What is the other number?  
*(Hint: for two numbers, GCF × LCM = product of the numbers.)*

**8.** A path is 3/4 mile long. Nina walks 2/5 of the path.  
How many miles does she walk? Give your answer as a fraction in lowest terms.
