# Target Set 3 (4 pairs)

**Rules:** 6 minutes per pair. Calculator OK. +10 each. Simplify fully.

---

## Pair 1 (Questions 1–2) — 6 minutes

**1.** A garden is 20 feet long and 12 feet wide.  
A fence costs $6 per foot. How much does it cost to fence the entire garden (in dollars)?

**2.** What is 3/8 of 160?

---

## Pair 2 (Questions 3–4) — 6 minutes

**3.** The average of five numbers is 18. Four of the numbers are 12, 20, 15, and 22.  
What is the fifth number?

**4.** A cube has edges of length 5 cm.  
What is the volume of the cube, in cubic centimeters?

---

## Pair 3 (Questions 5–6) — 6 minutes

**5.** A store discounts a $80 jacket by 25%.  
What is the sale price in dollars?

**6.** Two angles of a triangle measure 55° and 70°.  
What is the measure of the third angle, in degrees?

---

## Pair 4 (Questions 7–8) — 6 minutes

**7.** A recipe needs 2.5 cups of flour for 1 cake.  
How many cups of flour are needed for 6 cakes?

**8.** The sum of the digits of a two-digit number is 11.  
The tens digit is 3 more than the ones digit. What is the number?
