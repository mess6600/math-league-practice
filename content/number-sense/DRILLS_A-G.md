**Rules:** No calculator. No scratch paper. 8–10 minutes (Drill G: 5 minutes).  
Write only the answer. Every 10th problem is an **estimate** — answer must be an integer within 5% of the exact value.

Score practice sets: +5 correct, −4 wrong/blank from #1 through last blank you filled.
