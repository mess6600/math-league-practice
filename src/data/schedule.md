## How each round is scored (match contest day)

| Round | Time | Rules | Scoring |
|-------|------|-------|---------|
| **Number Sense** | 10 min | Mental only — no calc, no scratch | +5 correct, −4 wrong/blank through last attempted |
| **Sprint** | 40 min | 30 multiple choice, no calculator | +4 correct, −1 wrong, **0 for skip** |
| **Target** | 6 min × 4 pairs | Calculator OK | +10 per correct, no penalty |

**Individual total** = Target + Sprint + (Number Sense ÷ 4)  
(Negative Number Sense counts as 0)

## Parent tips (from his last scores)

1. **Number Sense:** Stop when accuracy drops. Going deeper with wrong blanks hurts (−4 each).
2. **Sprint:** Skipping beats guessing. Wrong = −1; skip = 0.
3. **Target:** His strength (5/8 last time). Keep doing timed pairs — don’t neglect it.
4. After every set: sort misses into *careless / didn’t know / out of time*. Only teach the “didn’t know” pile.
