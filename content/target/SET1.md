# Target Set 1 (4 pairs)

**Rules:** 6 minutes per pair. Calculator allowed. +10 per correct. Fully simplify. No units.

---

## Pair 1 (Questions 1–2) — 6 minutes

**1.** A bakery sold 48 muffins in the morning and 36 in the afternoon.  
If muffins are sold in boxes of 12, how many full boxes were sold in all?

**2.** The sum of three consecutive whole numbers is 78.  
What is the largest of the three numbers?

---

## Pair 2 (Questions 3–4) — 6 minutes

**3.** A rectangle is 18 cm long and 7 cm wide.  
What is the area of the rectangle, in square centimeters?

**4.** Priya ran 2.5 km on Monday, 3 km on Tuesday, and 1.5 km on Wednesday.  
What was her average (mean) distance per day, in kilometers?

---

## Pair 3 (Questions 5–6) — 6 minutes

**5.** A school has 240 students. 35% of them play a sport.  
How many students play a sport?

**6.** A square and a rectangle have the same perimeter of 40.  
The rectangle’s length is 14. What is the side length of the square?

---

## Pair 4 (Questions 7–8) — 6 minutes

**7.** Tickets cost $8 for adults and $5 for children.  
A family buys 2 adult and 3 child tickets. How much do they pay in dollars?

**8.** The product of two numbers is 72. One number is 8.  
What is the other number?
