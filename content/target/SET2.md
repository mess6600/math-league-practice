# Target Set 2 (4 pairs)

**Rules:** 6 minutes per pair. Calculator OK. +10 each. Simplify fully.

---

## Pair 1 (Questions 1–2) — 6 minutes

**1.** A tank holds 45 liters of water. It is 4/5 full.  
How many liters of water are in the tank?

**2.** Find the positive difference between 15² and 10².

---

## Pair 2 (Questions 3–4) — 6 minutes

**3.** A pizza is cut into 12 equal slices. Four friends each eat 2 slices.  
What fraction of the pizza is left? Give your answer in lowest terms.

**4.** The perimeter of a regular hexagon is 54 cm.  
What is the length of one side, in centimeters?

---

## Pair 3 (Questions 5–6) — 6 minutes

**5.** Books cost $12 each. Notebooks cost $3 each.  
Liam buys 4 books and 5 notebooks. What is the total cost in dollars?

**6.** A number is tripled, then 7 is added. The result is 40.  
What was the original number?

---

## Pair 4 (Questions 7–8) — 6 minutes

**7.** A car travels 55 miles per hour for 3 hours.  
How many miles does it travel?

**8.** In a class of 28 students, the ratio of boys to girls is 3:4.  
How many girls are in the class?
